About

The woman behind
the knots

Hi, I'm Kerryn. I turn yarn into things that make people say "oh wow, you made that?" — and yes, it took longer than you think.

What started as a way to keep my hands busy turned into a full-blown obsession with fibre, texture, and making beautiful things from scratch. Every piece is handmade by me — no machines, no shortcuts, just hours of work and an unreasonable amount of yarn.

I specialise in crochet and macramé pieces for your home, your wardrobe, and that awkward wall that needs something.
