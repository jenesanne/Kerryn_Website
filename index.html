About

The woman behind
the knots

Hi, I'm Kerryn. I turn yarn into things that make people say "oh wow, you made that?" — and yes, it took longer than you think.

What started as a way to keep my hands busy turned into a full-blown obsession with fibre, texture, and making beautiful things from scratch. Every piece is handmade by me — no machines, no shortcuts, just hours of work and an unreasonable amount of yarn.

I specialise in crochet and macramé pieces for your home, your wardrobe, and that awkward wall that needs something.

FAQ

Questions people ask
before they order

Why is it priced like this?

Because it's handmade. You're paying for skilled time, sore hands, and years of getting good at this.

Can I get it cheaper?

Not from me. I don't discount the hours it takes to make quality work, but mass-produced options are always out there.

Are these machine made?

Not even slightly. Every stitch and knot is made by hand in Cape Town, with snacks and questionable playlists.

Do you do custom orders?

Yes. Send your idea, size, and colors and I'll tell you the lead time, the price, and whether your vision is chaos or genius.
